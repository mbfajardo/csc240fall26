Beauty & Fragrance Store Inventory and Customer Inquiry System

CSC240 – Initial Project Proposal

  1. Application Overview

    For my project, I would like to create a database for an online beauty and fragrance store. The store would include products such as perfumes, makeup, skincare, and haircare.

    The purpose of the database is to help keep track of products, available inventory, and customer questions. Customers would be able to ask questions about products, such as their prices or whether they are in stock. Store employees would be able to add new products, update inventory, and respond to customer questions.

    I want this database to make it easier to organize beauty products and manage customer interactions in one place.

  2. Entities

    Some of the entities I plan to include are:

      Products: Stores information about products, including names, descriptions, prices, and categories.

      Brands: Keeps track of the different beauty and fragrance brands sold in the store.

      Inventory: Keeps track of how many products are available and whether they are in stock.

      Customers: Stores basic customer information, such as names and contact details.

      Messages: Keeps track of questions customers send and the responses from store employees.

      Employees: Stores information about employees who manage products and respond to customer questions.

  3. Business Processes

    Some of the things this database should be able to do include:
    
      Adding New Products: Employees can add new beauty and fragrance products to the database, including their names, brands, categories, and prices.
    
      Checking Product Availability: Customers or employees can check if a product is in stock and see how many are available.
    
      Updating Inventory: Employees can update product quantities when new products arrive or when items are sold.
    
      Customer Questions: Customers can send messages asking about products, prices, and availability.
    
      Responding to Customers: Employees can view customer questions and send responses.
    
      Searching for Products: Customers or employees can search for products by name, brand, category, or price.

  4. Project Scope

    For now, I plan to focus on organizing products, keeping track of inventory, and managing customer questions and responses. I may add more features as I learn more and get a better understanding of the project requirements.

  5. Project Status

    This project is currently in the planning stage. I will make changes and add more details as I continue working on it throughout the course.
